## Notes/Synopsis/Recap for session 3:
Lilania had just arrived with her guards as our intrepid adventurers were fighting to hold off the attacking cultists. Due to Artemis' and the teams valiant efforts to stop them, a few were managed to be captured while the rest however fled deeper into the tunnels. 
The group considered following said cultists, but in their current state (as some members were only just brought up from downed), thought it wise not to. 


The adventurers talked with Lilania briefly, in which time they were given their reward (25g) as well  as a couple of other items as Lilania realised how bad it was Artemis had to run backwards and forwards to talk with her in the Academy. *{The first trinket was a intricately carved **Minor Sending Crystal** tried to one on Lilania's person. The other item was a **Shadow-Bleed Orbuculum** - an opaque white crystal that blackens the nearer to "Odd" magic it is.}*
Lilania quickly wrapped up the conversation however as she told the group to go rest as they looked very much not in great shape. She tells the group to meet her here again at the next day as she has another job that she would like them to do, very much implying they would be going after said cultists. 
Exhausted, incredibly bloodied, but triumphant, the party went their separate ways to go sleep. On the way back to his place, John Goblin kept thinking he saw something out the corner of his eye. 

The next morning, the group begins to reconvene at the Cultists hideout. On the way there through the coins, John and Hai both notice someone is definitely following them. A hooded figure with a long black flowing cloak *(similar to who was following Artemis the previous night)*. John and Hai both duck into an alleyway to try and ambush said figure. The figure instead blurs past them, both barely catching a glimpse. As John and Hai try to chase said figure, the figure  dips into an alley of their own. As Hai and John enter the Alley, the figure is nowhere to be seen, only some footprints were left behind that disappeared midway down the alley. On investigation, Hai's magic begins to act up and they find another hidden entrance similar to the one in the coins. They use the Minor Sending Crystal to contact Lilania, who thanks them and tells them she will send someone to section it off, she beckons them to meet her as she sorely needs them to do this next task.

The party all arrive at the Cultist hideout, which is now absolutely bustling. The whole street is guarded and sectioned off from the public, with notable presence of the Token Guard (The District Watch forces for the Coins), Starwatch (Absaloms military Intelligence and investigation), and multiple academies pupils and professors. Desks have been moved down here to allow professors (such as Lilania) which house a large splurge of annotations, open books and papers.
Dancer aptly notes to John and Hai "Did you use the crystal in the alleyway?" and Hai, John, Dancer and Ivan all hurry back to said alleyway and pull out the Orbuculum - which it very quickly becomes dark grey. Intrigued, Hai moved it around the alleyway - noting it got very slightly darker in some areas of the alley, where for example some notable footprints were, but otherwise nothing.

--

When they return to the cultist hideout, they meet with Lilania. Lilania quickly greets the group thanking them for coming so quickly, and notes Artemis' absence is due to a tummy ache which is "Unusual for a Yaoguai but I didn't want to drag her out here if she wasnt feeling well." 

Lilania speaks with the party finally, with her very baggy eyes and stifled yawns. She begins to go through the notes and such she had made on her desk as she converses with the group.
Main points she says:
- The captured cultists were moved to a more secure location and are being interrogated by people better at that than her and these professors. 
- "The cultists have been incredibly tight lipped so far, so the party could talk to them if they wished, but even our best interrogators haven't gotten anything out of them."
  There were other things of note however; their clothing was essentially non-descript, and each held scriptures belonging to one of three gods - Rovagug, Lamashtu, or the Whispering Tyrant (Tar-Baphon). Lilania and the group conversed over what that means, as well as what each of those gods represented and she mentions "while from what we know of these cults, they dont fight eachother unless provoked, it definitely is concerning and odd (to our understanding) that they potentially are working together... Or someone wants it to look like they are"
- "The bodies were killed in a variety of ways. Some were mauled/bashed, others were sacrificially cut and bled, some had their important organs harvested (for seemingly powerful potions/drugs), and the rest were killed in minimal ways so as to (seemingly) preserve the body to best be resurrected or reused in another way."
	- "The oldest bodies are maybe a month or so old, maybe older, but they're unsure as of yet. The rooms have been subtly reinforced physically and with magic to make them cold and airtight enough that they do not decay nearly as quickly as they should. For example, it should smell a lot worse down here."
- "Yes we asked immediately if we could speak with the dead, but the beurocracy of it all means we'll be waiting days if not weeks to get proper approval for it."

Afterwards, Lilania asks the group to go and tackle the large group of cultists. She explains they had sent teams to scout further ahead, and they have come across sewers and what looks like it is maybe their base - but they couldn't risk engaging or being seen. 
Lilania explains to contact her with the Minor messaging crystal if they do find a base of cultists so she can direct any nearby groups to go help.

--

The group then goes off down the wooden corridor leading into darkness. They travel down it for many minutes before it changes to sewer stone as  it seems the hideout was somehow "carved out" past the normal sewers. The group continues down the sewers, then comes into a 3 pronged fork. 
The group chooses the leftmost fork, after which they eventually descend into sewers with running water as they make their way further inward. 

The group comes to a large intersection of sewers {Map 1}. The group moves through, seeing two guards who are talking to eachother with a lit torch. 
Ivan tries to distract said guards with an arrow, however they don't react. Ivan also notices their lights are not flickering or casting enough light as they should be. He and Dancer come to the conclusion they are illusions, and move ahead. 

There is a fork, and the group opts to go continue straight down the cobbled stone instead of towards large metallic pipes. As they do - they come back into the same area, and with the same illusory guards. The party also had to overcome a sudden sense of nausea and a sense of dread like one was falling. 
The players continue through this intersection of sewers again, they then saw that this 'third' iteration had real guards who were talking not-weather talk. Agreeing that these probably weren't illusions - Ivan stays while Dancer goes backwards to fetch the others, but not before he draws an intricate lion on a wall using chalk. Dancers drawing of a lion appeared faintly but distorted in the previous version, but the animal image he drew there did not appear in the final area.

The party groups up and prepares to take on the cultist guards. Ivan expertly knocking the torch out of ones hand with a shot, then skewering him through the neck with the second. Terrified, his comrade yells out "pls stop! I have kids! I did this for my girl Nancy - I needed the healthcare!" in surrender. He is tied up, and interrogated.
The group discovers:
- His name is Erwieyn and from his documents he is born in 4495. He doesnt know the year is 4726.
- The area he is from is nearby, with a small group of cultists. To what god, he couldnt say exactly. 
- The 'sect leader' - "Feldar Re'Tret", is a tan Elven with long black hair and a magic user. "He is the leader of our sect, and he does magic and stuff. Honestly a fair amount of our cult does magic, but that's expected for Absalom. I'm one of the boring ones."
- Some non-cultists (like wives and kids) do live there as well. 
- He has been on guard for a few hours, with replacements coming here to get them presumably in a few more hours. If they were not present, that would raise alarm bells.

The group decides with info to head out and investigate the hideout, while Hai stays behind with Erwieyn. They soldier forwards. 

--

The adventurers enter a large underground hideout inside the sewers, with wooden infastructure built on top of the canals along with multiple smaller rooms.
As they come up to the central round waterway, a loud voice can be hard giving a sermon. It fits the description of Feldar, "May this blood give our god the nourishment they need to grant us salvation!" His chanting is accompanied with quieter chants echoing his words, while other voices sob or cry out in despair.
The group get sight on what appears to be a large alter inside this central round chamber. There is Feldar, who is about to sacrifice a woman with carved runes on her skin, on an alter with similar looking runes.

Dancer leaps in and attacks to stop Feldars knife from hitting the hostage in their chest. 

The cultist initiates all cut themselves, in which their blood falls but then begins to be drawn towards Feldar as they attack to protect their leader. A cultist initiate sacrifices the first hostage, which seems to not work as well as it should've as Feldar lashes out in rage, killing said cultist. The two nearby adept cultists begin to fire spells towards Ivan.

As the first hostage dies, even though it didnt seem to be quite as Feldar wished, the ground begins to shudder and the lights in the area flicker as Feldar continues to chant as the sacrifice (and the murdered cultist) both bleed out.

To prevent any more sacrifices, Roy continually grapples Feldar, which is infurating to Feldar and the cultists all try to help him escape, but to no real avail. Feldar is cut down by Dancer, Ivan and John before the second sacrifice dies.

Dancer, Ivan and John strike down Feldar. As Feldar dies, the blood leaks out of his skin, forming the runes backwards then slowly seeping out of his skin back into the group. The flickering lights, weightlessness and shaking all begin to cease. As the adventurers strike down the remaining cultists, one adept is intimidated to surrender, while another initiate broke and ran through one of the tunnels. 

The group begin helping the dying hostages and start to talk to the remaining non-combatant cultist husbands, wives and teenagers.
