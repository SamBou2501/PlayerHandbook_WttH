## Notes/Synopsis/Recap for session 1:

The party of adventurers makes their way westward after being given instructions by [Lilania Viss](https://www.legendkeeper.com/a/cmsbss1rm0olw42lph3nxb1j5/cku5ofhyf00an03ccz90xg2ss) to go directly westward until they "Get to where they need to go".  
The group travels via the main roads across the surrounding Blasted Lands and into Immenwood at a respectable speed. Passerbys are moving with them to and fro Absalom.

Near the end of their first day of travel, the group comes along the a major river which has a large fortified bridge going over it. The bridge is however noticably deserted - with no guards or other soul in sight.  
The group splits up looking for any clues of the guards whereabouts or what happened here, however they all turn up relatively empty handed (They found some tracks, leading off into the wood, but lost them immidiately).  
Hai Howryu notices his magic works very haphazardly here, and this leads him to investigate underneath the bridge itself.  
Beneath the bridge, Hai's ancestors talk to him through 'chopped together sentences' - making sense, but scrambled between different points in any given sentence. And Ivan Namscar's whispers increase in number but do not become scrambled like Hai's ancestors.  
The group, being creeped out (and not know where the nearest guardpost would be) opts to continue westward towards their overall objective.

They quickly decide to camp as night approaches, picking a spot maybe 100m from the main road. A few adventurers experience very odd phenomena as they do their watch shift, ranging from seeing shadowy versions of the party to climbing up a tree taking vastly longer than it should have.  
As the party departs the next day and goes back to the main road - they notice that it takes nearly an hour to get back to the main road, when they could've sworn they camped within 100m of the main road the night before.  
This second day they notice almost noone coming from the other direction of the road, which puts them on edge.  
After a few hours of travel, Ivan and Dancer both hear commotion (of the not good kind) up ahead, and get the party into fighting formation as they hurry forward. Several minutes later they come up to a fork in the main road that has a dreary scene.

The group explores the area to investgiate the bodies, the barricade, the odd gravity fields, the floating bodies, the aged bodies, the old/decayed trees, and the large holes exposing dark rocks. A couple of party members (including artemis) go into of the odd gravity fields (which Artemis behaves oddly in). They identify the floating bodies are in fact seemingly alive, if comatose of a sort, and decide to tie them all (still floating) to a still-working cart.  
As they are tying the last bodies to the cart, Dancer notices blooming yellow flowers at one edge of the clearing. Artemis goes to inspect them, when the body reaches up to grab her with its viny floral arm. The group of adventureres quickly dismember th plant and its infested hosts. Artemis identifies the plant and bodies (A Yellow Musk Creeper with its Yellow Musk Zombies), and notes that there is no way it should be this inland as well as how did it take root so quickly if this whole scene happened in a matter of minutes?

The group then departs - continuing west towards their goal.

As the group approaches the town they were pointed to, it is eeriely quiet, and they have noticed no travellers coming from the east. This prompts Ivan and Dancer get to go ahead into the town to scout around.  
They find the town is seemingly deserted - but with even more floating people and debris at many points of the town above similar looking 'grey rock holes' with more odd gravity fields.  
As they begin to explore buildings, Ivan stumbles across guards in a guard post that are asleep and have been seemingly asleep for a very long time. Dancer checked a few buildings but found noone.

After getting a bit spooked, Dancer decides to draw anything hiding out into the open, by banging two pans together. This draws out odd looking zombies who look at the two adventurers, as at the same time two translucent creatures who were completely silent also slowly came into view but did not directly react to their presence.  
Ivan and Dancer BOOKED IT and the party gathered at the eastern entrance to the large village to create a very good defensive choke against these foes as more zombies appeared from around the town.  
The party valiantly fought at the chokepoint to not allow the weird dogs and their zombies to surround them - but had to split once the dogs decided to instead use the no-gravity areas to float up and bite more people. However the party are victorious against these enemies, with more than a few bruises.

With the dogs and zombies dead, the party more thoroughly scouted the town and find many more asleep people inside previously empty houses.  
When John Goblin tried to open the door to the mage tower, the protective sigils and runes in the town 'popped' and dispersed throughought the town as a shimmer of magic pulsed towards the mage tower.

The people who were asleep began to groggily wake, among them being the Wizards and other magic users who were residing inside the Mage Tower itself.  
John Goblin is greeted by frantic apprentices who begin sending messages via a Communication Crystal while another fetches the Arch-wizard and his fellow colleagues. The Arch-Wizard introduces himself as [Malifikar](https://www.legendkeeper.com/a/cmsbss1rm0olw42lph3nxb1j5/cku5q5dp800hy03cc48pouqy2) and begins to talk with the party privately after he verified them by the sigil on the letter Ivan was slipped by Lilania.

He revealed that he put up the protective wards and runes around the town to put it into stasis, with a failsafe that if (a sentient) someone tried to enter the mage tower it would cause them to disperse that. John Goblin aptly noted that got around this with his construct which frazzled Malifikar.  
Malifikar continued to explain that the town had been experiencing multiple types of 'anomolies' before he got a message from the future detailing "the events of a tragic event that utterly destroyed Woodfall".  
When he felt the the destruction of multiple buildings and abberations appearing in the town, he reflecively activated the stasis wards to protect who he could.  
He revealed that he thought the year was 4714 (you know the current year is 4726) - it seems he had been in stasis for over a decade.

Malifikar said he doesnt know Lilania personally, and doesnt know about artemis as they were both before when he went into stasis, but he does have theories on why she was sent here with what he had seen. He sternly dismised your attempts to learn more about the group the Sigil represents, and told the group to go back to this "lilania" that sent them to get more answers.

Before Malifikar dismisses the party, he promises he would write a report for the group to take back to Absalom (with quick transport) and he thanks them for saving the town. He then turns around and starts yelling at apprentices to "NOT use that spell in the gravity fields!" The town began to bustle to life as apprencices unsoiled trousers, civillians tended to the floating people, and people of all types began to put the village back in order.
