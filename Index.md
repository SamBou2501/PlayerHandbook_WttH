Welcome to Wider Than The Hem! This is my (Samuel) homebrew cosmic horror campaign for PF2E.

# Campaign main themes:

- **Cosmic Horror**: Fear of the unknowable, fear of the unfathomably powerful, and the discomfort/fear of the harsh uncaringness and inhumanity of the universe.
	-  More knowledge is generally bad and horrifying and in most cases, comprehending things is not possible 
	- Everything leads to more questions. 
	- True comprehension drives you insane.
- (At times) **Grim dark, hopelessness**

## _(Balanced with)_

- **Unity**: many people coming together for Common goals and working for the ‘common good’.
- **Hope and perseverance**: While things seem bad, and get worse, the PCs are still actively helping and see how it is helping
- some **Absurdity and light-heartedness** (this is still a tabletop RPG)

# _General gameplay vibes_

- **Combat**: lots of beating up monsters and corrupted things (but ultimately up to you)
- **Investigating**: LOTS of piecing together information and following leads/clues
- **Planning**: a fair amount of planning your next moves with available information. (You can go ‘flying by the seat of your pants’ if you wish though, you may just miss stuff)
