A naive yaoguai that was a helmet which belonged to (supposedly) an important historical general from Tian Xia. She is a Commander named Artemis Heartly. The tomb she was residing in was raided and was donated back to this academy as a gift many years later. She was put in an enchanted display shelf and slumbered for many years; until at some point she awoke and transformed behind the glass. A flabbergasted janitor found her stuck behind the glass, newly awakened “WHAT? HOW did you get past the warded glass?? GUARDS!”

She then gained a scholarship at the academy and began studying military tactics at the “Outstanding Military Academy of Absalom”. She was regularly visited by important Tian Xia scholars and mages as she studied (because she is just THAT amazing). Her retained memories/intuition helps with her assignments, not so much with the more practical training. She will be sent out of the academy for the first time since her awakening to go improve her practical skills and test her theories and to help get some extra credits.
