
Intricately carved crystal with patterns refracting and forming a symbol of a closed eye inside of it. The eye opens when in use. 
The holder of the crystal hears a chime in their mind and the following message from the person speaking it. They can replay said message for all to hear. 

*Thrice per day* - Somatically Activate the item for 1 minute, sending messages to the other holder of this crystal. Messages are always sent, even if they are sometimes 'delayed' due to where you/the other holder are currently located. 

Is considered magical. Does NOT need to be attuned. Cost unknown.
