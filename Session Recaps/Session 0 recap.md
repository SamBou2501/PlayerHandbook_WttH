## Notes/Synopsis/Recap for session 0:

Ivan (Victor) and Dancer (Sean) who were both inside the shadow Plane/Netherworld, were making their way to the Absalom gate, when they were both shunted abruptly to Absalom after the gate stuttered. They weren't the only ones - but on the other side, the gate seemed to be working fine again. 

Dancer latched onto Ivan as Ivan didn't shrug him off. Perturbed, but also both needing gold, they look for work and stumble across a very... peculiar poster of many self-sketches of a Woman with deer horns, accompanied with a long blurb about herself. Intrigued and hoping it's a bounty, they ask nearby guards where to go since it doesn't actually say a location - and they direct them to a nearby tavern. 

Hai Howryu (Iain) at this time was VERY badly needing food, and some gold, and similarly follows the posters to the same tavern. 

Artemis (Carly) was told by one of the Headmistress' to gather a group of adventurers, and so she made those posters hoping people would wanna join her at said tavern. As the other three enter, they very quickly become acquainted, and join the would be martial training expedition. Back at the academy a headmistress - [Lilania Viss](https://www.legendkeeper.com/a/cmsbss1rm0olw42lph3nxb1j5/cku5ofhyf00an03ccz90xg2ss) (a professor of arcane military stratagem) welcomes the... diverse group of adventurers, and gives them potential locations to go investigate "odd occurrences". 

After Lilania gives very vague and unhelpful extra information about the task, the group choose the closest location - a spot in the nearby verdant forests and fields just west of Absalom (a days travel, in Immenwood).  Lilania did say though just "due west and you will find it". _Before they take off, Artemis goes to the library and expertly copies a military map of the area so they actually know where they are going._

On the trip there Ivan (Victor) realises there was a letter inside his pocket, the haunting voices he hears begin whispering when he goes to open the letter. The letter read:

> _“Artemis must come into contact with the anomaly” “Protect Artemis at all costs. Bring her back safely."_

Signed with a very prominent but unidentifiable (by Ivan) sigil. After reading the letter, the haunting voices begin to scream and cause Ivan severe disorientation (Victor failed the whispers save when he read the letter. They were telling him to NOT read it).
